Good questions

Answers before you ask

Still unsure? Call (512) 555-0147 — a real person picks up.

Is Signal Wireless the same company as Verizon? +

No. Signal Wireless is an independent, locally owned authorized retailer of Verizon products and services. We help you choose and activate Verizon plans and devices, but we are a separate business from Verizon Communications Inc.

Do I pay more by buying through a retailer? +

No. You pay standard Verizon plan pricing. As an authorized retailer we simply add local, face-to-face help with setup, trade-ins and data transfer at no added cost to you.

Can I trade in my current phone? +

Yes. Bring in an eligible device and we'll appraise it on the spot. Approved trade-in credit is applied toward a new phone or as account credit, subject to Verizon's current promotions and eligibility rules.

Do you set up Home Internet and Fios? +

Yes. We check availability at your address for Verizon 5G Home, LTE Home and Fios fiber, then help you order the plan that fits your household.

What do I need to bring to switch? +

A photo ID, your current phone (if you're trading it in or porting your number), and your account details from your current carrier if you'd like to keep your number. We'll handle the rest.
