Privacy & Data Protection

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This notice explains how Signal Wireless handles your information. It is provided as a general template and should be reviewed by qualified counsel and tailored to your jurisdiction before publication.

Signal Wireless ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent authorized retailer of Verizon products and services. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This notice describes what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Signal Wireless is a separate, locally owned business and is not Verizon Communications Inc. When you purchase or activate Verizon service through us, Verizon also processes your information under its own privacy policy. This notice covers only our own practices as a retailer.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us — such as your name, phone number, email, and address when you request a quote or visit our store.
  • Service interest — the plans, devices, or internet options you ask us about.
  • Transaction information needed to help you activate service or process an order. Full payment card numbers are handled through secure, PCI-DSS-compliant processors and are not stored on our own systems.
  • Website usage data — basic analytics such as pages viewed and general location, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookies Policy).

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To help you select, order, and activate Verizon plans, devices, and internet service.
  • To provide support before and after your purchase.
  • To send you information you request or agree to receive (see our Marketing Policy and TCPA Policy).
  • To improve our website and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We share information only as needed to serve you: with Verizon to activate and support the services you request; with trusted service providers (such as payment processors and analytics tools) bound by confidentiality obligations; and where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or limit the use of your personal information, and to opt out of certain data sharing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

6. How we protect your data

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. Card payments are processed through PCI-DSS-compliant providers (see our PCI DSS statement). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and limit access to those who need it.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, then delete or de-identify it.

8. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Reach us at privacy@signalwireless-example.com, call (512) 555-0147, or write to Signal Wireless, 1200 Commerce Avenue, Suite 5, Austin, TX 78701.
