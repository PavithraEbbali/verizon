This Cookies Policy explains how Signal Wireless uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It should be read alongside our Privacy & Data Protection notice.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use it. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage, and tags.

2. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the website to function — for example, remembering your cookie choices and keeping the site secure. These cannot be switched off in our systems.

Performance & analytics

Help us understand which pages are popular and how the site performs, so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Functional

Remember choices you make (such as your region) to give you a more personalized experience.

Marketing (optional)

If enabled, these help measure the effectiveness of our advertising. We only set them with your consent where required.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third parties we use, such as analytics providers or embedded content. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control third-party cookies set by external sites you may visit through links on our site.

4. Managing your preferences

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings, and where we provide a consent banner, you can update your choices at any time. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works. For guidance, see your browser's help pages or visit a resource such as your local data-protection authority's website.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no common industry standard for responding to these signals, our site does not currently respond to them, but we honor the cookie choices you make here.

6. Updates

We may update this policy as our site or the law changes. The "last updated" date shows the most recent revision.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies? Email privacy@signalwireless-example.com.
