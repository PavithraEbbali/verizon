Service Fulfillment

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This page explains how Signal Wireless fulfills the products and services you order. Because we are an authorized Verizon retailer, some steps are completed together with Verizon under Verizon's official terms.

1. What we fulfill

  • Activation of new Verizon mobile lines and plan changes.
  • Sale and setup of smartphones, tablets, wearables, hotspots, and accessories.
  • Ordering and coordination of Verizon Home Internet and Fios where available.
  • Trade-in appraisal and application of eligible credit.

2. Order & activation timeline

In-store activations are usually completed the same day, typically within about 30–60 minutes depending on number porting and account verification. Home Internet and Fios installation or shipping timelines are set by Verizon and depend on your address and product; we will give you an estimated date when you order.

3. Delivery & pickup

Devices purchased in store are provided at the time of sale, subject to stock. For items we order on your behalf, delivery estimates are provided at checkout. Please inspect your device before leaving or upon delivery and contact us right away if anything is missing or damaged.

4. Payments

We accept major payment methods in store. Device-payment and financing options are provided by Verizon or its financing partners under their terms and eligibility checks. Card payments are processed securely (see our PCI DSS statement).

5. Returns, exchanges & cancellations

Returns and exchanges follow Verizon's official return policy, including any applicable return window and restocking fees. Bring your receipt and original packaging. Some promotional items and opened accessories may be non-returnable. We will explain the specific terms that apply to your purchase at the time of sale.

6. Warranties & support

Devices carry the manufacturer's warranty, and optional protection plans may be available through Verizon. For service or network issues, we help you troubleshoot and, when needed, coordinate with Verizon support. You are always welcome to return to our store for hands-on help.

7. Contact

Questions about an order? Email orders@signalwireless-example.com or call (512) 555-0147.
