TCPA Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This template describes how we handle calls and texts under the U.S. Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). Consult counsel to confirm it fits your calling/texting practices and any state rules.

Signal Wireless respects your communication preferences and complies with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and related regulations. This policy explains how we obtain consent to contact you and how you can opt out.

1. Your consent

When you submit a form, provide your number, or check a consent box on our website, you agree that Signal Wireless may contact you by phone call or text message about your request — including through automated technology — at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Standard message and data rates may apply.

2. What we may send

  • Responses to your quote requests and questions.
  • Appointment reminders and order or activation updates.
  • Occasional offers and promotions, where you have agreed to receive them.

3. How to opt out

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. To stop calls, tell the representative you wish to be placed on our internal do-not-call list, or email us at the address below. For help, reply HELP to a text or contact us directly. Opting out of marketing messages will not stop transactional messages needed to complete a request you initiated.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us. We do not sell your phone number to third parties for their own marketing.

5. Our records

We maintain records of consent and opt-out requests. If you believe you received a message in error, contact us and we will promptly investigate and honor your preferences.

6. Carrier notice

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan.

7. Contact

To manage your preferences, email optout@signalwireless-example.com or call (512) 555-0147.
