Marketing Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This policy states our advertising standards. It is designed to align with common advertising-platform rules (including Google Ads' misrepresentation and third-party-reseller policies). Have counsel confirm it fits your campaigns.

Signal Wireless is committed to honest, transparent marketing. As an independent authorized Verizon retailer, we hold our advertising to high standards so that customers always understand who we are and what they are buying.

1. Truthful & clear

  • We describe our business accurately and never imply that we are Verizon Communications Inc.
  • Every ad and landing page identifies Signal Wireless as an independent authorized retailer.
  • We do not use misleading claims, fake urgency, or "bait-and-switch" tactics.

2. Accurate offers & pricing

Prices, promotions, and eligibility we advertise reflect Verizon's official terms at the time of publication. Because Verizon can change these, we present figures as illustrative and confirm final pricing at the point of sale. Material conditions (such as trade-in or plan requirements) are disclosed clearly.

3. Responsible brand use

We use Verizon and other trademarks only to identify genuine products we are authorized to sell, consistent with applicable brand guidelines and our Trademarks notice. We do not bid on or present brand terms in a way designed to confuse consumers about who operates this website.

4. Permission-based outreach

We contact people only in line with our TCPA Policy and Privacy notice. Consent is never a condition of purchase, and every marketing message includes a clear way to opt out.

5. Advertising-platform compliance

Our digital advertising is designed to comply with the policies of the platforms we use, including rules on misrepresentation, transparency for third-party resellers, and accurate business information. We keep landing pages consistent with our ads and maintain functional contact details.

6. Inclusive & accessible

We aim to make our marketing accessible and respectful, avoiding discriminatory content and following accessibility best practices on our website.

7. Contact

Questions or feedback about our marketing? Email marketing@signalwireless-example.com.
